## 🧠 About Me

I am an **AI/ML Engineer** with a deep passion for **Generative AI**, **Large Language Models (LLMs)**, and solving complex business problems through data-driven architectures. My focus is on crossing the chasm between experimental models and production-ready, scalable AI systems. 

- 🔭 **Expertise:** Machine Learning, Deep Learning, NLP, Computer Vision, and AI System Design.
- 💡 **Passion:** Designing Agentic AI, optimizing RAG pipelines, and fine-tuning state-of-the-art foundation models.
